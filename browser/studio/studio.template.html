Arena & Raid

Global controls affect every account on the selected server.

Arena season controls

Changing the season number keeps standings. Ending pauses new battles. Starting the next season settles ranking rewards to the Inbox (1st–3rd: featured 6★ limited ally, 4th–100th: 5★ version, 0~1% and below: Ally Ticket, plus Gems), archives standings, resets any ally pin to auto-cycle, and starts a fresh seven-day season.

Weekly raid controls

Changing boss or advancing starts a new raid and keeps previous scores. Refresh the game's Raid board after a change.

Pinned notices

Notices pinned at the top of chat right now. The game shows only the newest one; when it ends or is removed, the next one shows. Edits and removals reach browser players within 30 seconds without a new chat line; the Android app keeps a removed pin until it ends or the player reconnects.

Connect to load pinned notices.

Notices in chat history

Every [Notice] the server keeps in chat history (the last 25 lines per channel), newest first. Players get these lines again when they reload (F5), reconnect or change channel. Editing or deleting one changes what they get from then on; a line already in an open chat window stays there until that player reloads or reconnects, because the game keeps its own copy of the chat.
